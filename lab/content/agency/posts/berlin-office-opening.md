## Designing access around the work

A headless CMS needs to do more than deliver content to a website. Editors need appropriate access to their work, while sensitive actions and information need protection. Those decisions are easier to manage when roles and permissions are considered early.

For teams working with Payload CMS, we have documented [approaches to access control]({{site}}/blog/payload-access-control-patterns/). The article looks at patterns that can help developers plan who can read, create and change content.

## Making migrations manageable

Moving to a new CMS involves content as well as code. Teams need to account for existing pages, editorial workflows and the links that readers already use. A migration plan should give each of those areas attention before launch.

Our guide to [avoiding migration mistakes]({{site}}/blog/cms-migration-mistakes/) covers issues to consider when preparing a move. It is a starting point for conversations between developers, editors and the people responsible for the existing site.
