## Building for change

A headless CMS gives teams a way to manage content independently of the front end. That separation can be useful when a site needs to evolve, but it also calls for decisions about how pages are built, delivered and maintained.

In our account of [Kettlebrook Bank’s move of 1,400 pages without a traffic drop]({{site}}/case-studies/kettlebrook-bank/), the scale of that challenge is clear. A migration is not just a matter of moving content: teams also need to consider the pages visitors and search engines already rely on.
