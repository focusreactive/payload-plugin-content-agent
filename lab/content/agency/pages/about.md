=== richTextSection
heading: Building for the people behind the content

{{brand}} was founded in 2015. Since then, we have delivered 180 projects, helping organisations give their content a clear structure and a practical route to the screens where people need it.

A headless CMS separates content management from the presentation layer. That gives teams room to publish across channels while developers choose the tools that fit the experience. The separation also makes the content model important: editors need fields and relationships that reflect how they actually work, not just how a page looks today.

Our work brings those decisions together:

- Define content types and relationships around editorial needs.
- Build frontend experiences that use content consistently.
- Plan for changes such as new markets, languages and channels.

For a closer look at the modelling process, explore our [guide to structuring content]({{site}}/docs/guides/content-modeling/). It is part of the documentation for {{short}} Starter, our open source starter kit for Next.js and a headless CMS.

The starter gives teams a way to begin without treating every project as a blank slate. Version 3.2 is the current release, and developers can install it with `npx create-headless-starter`. From there, the work is about making the platform fit the organisation and its content.

=== twoColumnText
heading: How we work
left: We start by understanding the content and the people responsible for it. That means looking at what editors need to create, how content relates across the organisation and where it needs to appear. A useful model should make everyday publishing clearer while giving the frontend a dependable structure to work with. We make those decisions early, so the platform is shaped around the work it needs to support.
right: Design and engineering follow the same principle: build for the experience people need, without losing sight of the team maintaining it. We use tools such as Next.js and a headless CMS to separate presentation from content management. When a site needs another language, the process also needs to make sense for editors; our [guide to adding a locale]({{site}}/docs/recipes/add-a-locale/) explains one approach in {{short}} Starter.

=== featureGrid
