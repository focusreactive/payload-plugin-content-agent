=== richTextSection
heading: Know what to fix before you rebuild

A slow site or frustrating publishing workflow does not automatically mean you need a new CMS. The cause may be an oversized page, an inefficient content query or a model that forces editors to repeat work. Our audit separates those problems so you can make decisions based on evidence.

We start with your goals and inspect the setup you already have. That includes the CMS configuration, representative content types, publishing workflow and the way your site fetches and renders content. For performance, we use the data available to you alongside targeted page tests; we explain where the evidence is strong and where further investigation is needed.

Your report covers:

- Findings tied to specific parts of your setup
- Recommended actions, ordered by impact and effort
- Dependencies and questions to resolve before implementation
- A roadmap your team can use for planning

The audit is independent of a rebuild. You can use the recommendations with your existing team or ask {{brand}} to help implement them. We do not include implementation in the audit price, so the scope and cost of any follow-on work can be agreed separately.

For broader context on platform choices and architecture, read our analysis of the [state of headless CMS in 2026]({{site}}/blog/state-of-headless-cms-2026/).
