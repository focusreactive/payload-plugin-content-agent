=== richTextSection
heading: Make Payload work for your team

Payload is an open source, TypeScript-based CMS that puts content configuration in your codebase. Its admin panel runs on Next.js, and its APIs let you deliver content to a website or other applications. That makes it useful for teams that want an editorial interface without giving up control over how their content is structured and delivered.

We start by looking at what your editors publish, who needs access and which systems exchange data with the site. From there, we define collections and fields, configure permissions and build the frontend around the content people actually need to manage.

A typical engagement can include:

- Collection schemas for pages, articles and reusable content.
- Draft and version settings suited to your publishing process.
- A Next.js frontend connected to Payload.
- Migration scripts and checks for existing content.
- Deployment planning and an editorial handover.

If you are moving from WordPress, our [migration checklist]({{site}}/blog/wordpress-to-headless-migration-checklist/) covers the decisions to make before transferring content.

For projects that fit, we can also use {{short}} Starter 3.2, our open source starter kit for Next.js and a headless CMS. Install it with `npx create-headless-starter` to explore the starting point. We then adapt the implementation to your content model and project requirements.

- What is Payload CMS? | Payload is an open source, TypeScript-based content management system. It provides an admin panel and APIs for managing and delivering content.
- Does Payload require Next.js? | Payload’s admin panel is built on Next.js, and Payload can be added to a Next.js application. Its APIs can also deliver content to other frontends and applications.
