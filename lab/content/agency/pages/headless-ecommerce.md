=== imageWithText
heading: Put content and commerce in their own hands

Your commerce platform manages transactions and product data. A headless CMS gives editors a place to create landing pages, campaign content and other material that supports the sale. The storefront brings both together for the customer.

We start by mapping which system owns each piece of information, then design the content model and integrations around your team’s workflow. If your existing site runs on WordPress, we can help you [plan a move to a headless architecture]({{site}}/wordpress-to-headless-migration/).

For teams building with Next.js, our open source {{short}} Starter provides a starting point for a headless CMS project. Version 3.2 is installed with `npx create-headless-starter`; teams moving from version 2 can consult the [upgrade guide]({{site}}/docs/guides/upgrading-to-3/).

=== faqList
heading: Headless ecommerce questions
- What is headless ecommerce? | It separates the customer-facing storefront from the commerce platform that manages operations such as products and orders. The storefront connects to commerce services through APIs.
- Why use a CMS alongside a commerce platform? | A CMS gives editors tools to manage content such as campaign pages and editorial material. The commerce platform remains responsible for commerce data and transactions.
- Which commerce platform should we choose? | Shopify, Medusa and commercetools offer different levels of managed infrastructure and customization. The right choice depends on your requirements, team and existing systems.
- Can we keep our existing commerce platform? | Often, an existing platform can serve a new headless storefront through its APIs. We would assess its capabilities and your integration needs before recommending an approach.
- Can our editors update pages without developer help? | Reusable CMS components can let editors assemble and update pages within an agreed design system. Developers are still needed for new functionality or changes to the underlying components.
- How long does a project take? | Timing depends on the scope, integrations and readiness of your content. The stages above are indicative and give us a starting point for planning.
