=== richTextSection
heading: A better foundation for content and code

A headless CMS separates content management from the website frontend. Editors work in the CMS, while developers use its APIs to deliver content to the pages and applications your audience sees. That separation gives each team room to work without tying every content change to a frontend release.

{{brand}} starts by mapping the content you need to create, reuse and maintain. We define the relationships between content types, build the frontend around them and connect the services your website depends on. Before launch, we test the publishing workflow as well as the visitor experience.

Our work can include:

- Content models and editorial workflows designed around your team
- Frontend development with Next.js and integration with your chosen CMS
- Content migration, redirects and checks for search visibility
- Connections to search, commerce and other external services
- Testing, deployment and handover to your team

For a commerce site, the CMS is only one part of the stack. The product catalog, checkout and content experience also need to work together. Our guide to [choosing a headless commerce platform]({{site}}/blog/top-headless-commerce-platforms/) covers options to consider alongside your CMS.

The result is a website with a clear structure behind every page, so your editors can publish confidently and your developers can keep improving the experience.
